# NovelPaper - Project Completion Summary

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project successfully delivers a complete, artistic website celebrating the art of folding book pages. The website embodies minimalist elegance, literary reverence, and sustainable artistry.

## ✅ Completed Phases

### Phase 1: Brand & Concept Development ✓
**Website Name**: NovelPaper  
**Tagline**: "Where Stories Take Shape"  
**Brand Story**: Celebrates the delicate intersection of literature and sculpture, transforming discarded books into breathtaking art while preserving stories and giving them new dimensions.

**Artistic Philosophy** (5 Core Principles):
1. Minimalism with Meaning - Every fold serves a purpose
2. Respect for the Written Word - Transform with reverence
3. Transformation Through Patience - True art cannot be rushed
4. Beauty in Subtlety - The quietest statements are often the most profound
5. Sustainability Through Repurposing - Giving new life to old books
